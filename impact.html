Nonprofit Leadership

As Executive Director of KEEN New York, I helped grow free, volunteer-powered sports and recreation programs for children and young adults with disabilities across New York City.

Marathon Guide Runner

Guide runner with Achilles International, supporting athletes with visual impairments from local races to the TCS New York City Marathon. Every mile has shaped how I think about accessibility, trust, and human-centered design.

Movement & Teaching

Yoga — adaptive practice and community.

As a Registered Yoga Teacher with a focus on adaptive practice, the teaching work is about making movement accessible to bodies that don't fit the assumed template. Yoga's relationship with disability is complicated — the adaptive practice community is working to change that.

The body is always where the question of access becomes most concrete.

Community & Horticulture

Rooted in Central Park.

As a volunteer horticulturalist with the Central Park Conservancy, the work is quiet, seasonal, and grounding — tending one of the most visited public spaces in the world, one plant at a time.

Shared public space is its own kind of accessibility project. Everyone deserves somewhere beautiful to be.
