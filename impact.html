Adaptive Sports

Marathon guide running with Achilles International.

Guide running is a specific kind of trust. The guide and the athlete run together — tethered — sharing pace, course knowledge, and the physical experience of 26.2 miles. With Achilles International, this means guiding athletes with visual impairments through some of the world's most demanding races.

The embodied experience of navigating the world alongside someone who experiences it differently is not incidental to the technology and accessibility work. It is the source of it.

Running together
Movement & Teaching

Yoga — adaptive practice and community.

As a Registered Yoga Teacher with a focus on adaptive practice, the teaching work is about making movement accessible to bodies that don't fit the assumed template. Yoga's relationship with disability is complicated — the adaptive practice community is working to change that.

The body is always where the question of access becomes most concrete.
