About Me

I'm an author, technologist, and experience designer. Before returning to Columbia University to study computer science, robotics, and AI, I spent more than a decade in the music and live events industries, producing large-scale experiences, leading nonprofits, and creating opportunities for people to come together.

One event changed everything. Watching Stephen Hawking communicate through his assistive device completely shifted how I thought about what was possible. I stopped seeing it as something that simply made life easier and started seeing it as something that could remove barriers and create new possibilities. That moment is what led me to where I am today.

Today, whether I'm writing children's books, exploring robotics, or thinking about the future of AI, I'm drawn to projects that solve real problems and help people connect with the world around them.
