About Me

I'm an author, technologist, and experience designer. I produce live events, build community, and am completing a degree in computer science at Columbia University.

Watching Stephen Hawking communicate through assistive technology changed the course of my career. It sparked a curiosity that eventually led me back to Columbia.

Whether I’m writing books, exploring robotics, or thinking about the future of AI, I’m drawn to projects that solve real problems and expand what’s possible.

When I'm not working, you'll usually find me cheering on the Knicks, reading, sewing, tackling a difficult jigsaw puzzle, or convincing myself I have time for one more project.
